# Instacart

Instacart - Major LeetCode-style problems to drill for Instacart SWE interviews

Focus: heaps, routing-style graphs, intervals, sliding windows, arrays, greedy reasoning, and data-structure design.

These are not guaranteed exact interview questions. Treat this as the high-signal Instacart-style drill set: if you can solve these cleanly under time pressure, you are covering the main techniques Instacart-style live coding tends to reward.
