# Zillow

Zillow - Major LeetCode-style problems to drill for Zillow SWE interviews

Focus: search/ranking, heaps, intervals, grids, graph traversal, maps, trees, and DP.

These are not guaranteed exact interview questions. Treat this as the high-signal Zillow-style drill set: if you can solve these cleanly under time pressure, you are covering the main techniques Zillow-style live coding tends to reward.
