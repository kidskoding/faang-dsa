# Notion

Notion - Major LeetCode-style problems to drill for Notion SWE interviews

Focus: document-style data structures, tries, trees, graph traversal, strings, parsing, intervals, and DP.

These are not guaranteed exact interview questions. Treat this as the high-signal Notion-style drill set: if you can solve these cleanly under time pressure, you are covering the main techniques Notion-style live coding tends to reward.
