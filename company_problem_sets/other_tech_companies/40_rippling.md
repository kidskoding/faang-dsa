# Rippling

Rippling - Major LeetCode-style problems to drill for Rippling SWE interviews

Focus: file-system design, scheduling, graphs, intervals, strings, trees, heaps, and stateful implementation.

These are not guaranteed exact interview questions. Treat this as the high-signal Rippling-style drill set: if you can solve these cleanly under time pressure, you are covering the main techniques Rippling-style live coding tends to reward.
