# Airtable

Airtable - Major LeetCode-style problems to drill for Airtable SWE interviews

Focus: tables, formulas, graphs, design data structures, intervals, strings, heaps, trees, and DP.

These are not guaranteed exact interview questions. Treat this as the high-signal Airtable-style drill set: if you can solve these cleanly under time pressure, you are covering the main techniques Airtable-style live coding tends to reward.
